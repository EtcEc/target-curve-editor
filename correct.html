Correction

0 corrects the full range. Above 0, nothing changes below cutoff ÷ √2 and the correction fades in over the octave centred on the cutoff. Each curve keeps its level at 1 kHz.

What all speakers share in the bass goes to the sub, what one speaker has on its own goes to that speaker. Its level is kept where it was.

Detail is how wide an area the measured error is averaged over: small values follow narrower features, large values correct only broad trends. Strength is how much of the error is corrected. Limit caps how far each curve may end up from your target, over all rounds.
